# Between Us

## Register

product

## Users

Visitors exploring a macOS-like desktop simulation in the browser. They use familiar desktop apps such as Photos to browse, preview, and interact with the supplied content.

## Product Purpose

A personal macOS-inspired experience. Between Us recreates the feel of a desktop environment as an interactive browser experience. Success means visitors can understand the desktop quickly, open apps naturally, and complete lightweight interactions without encountering fake or confusing affordances.

## Brand Personality

Authentic macOS-like, restrained, polished, and task-focused. The interface should feel familiar before it feels novel.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-generated dashboard layouts.
- Decorative controls that do not communicate a real action.
- Runtime upload, cloud, or editing promises that the static GitHub Pages architecture cannot support.
- Overly saturated inactive states, gratuitous motion, and inconsistent icon or button vocabularies.

## Design Principles

- Preserve familiar desktop mental models.
- Make visible controls honest about their capabilities.
- Prefer focused, local interactions over speculative platform features.
- Keep visual hierarchy restrained so the content remains primary.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target a WCAG AA-oriented baseline with semantic HTML, keyboard access, visible focus states, responsive touch targets, descriptive labels, and reduced-motion support.
