Between Us

If you ever doubt how much I love you, just open this MacBook.
Look at everything inside it, because I made all of it for you
What this project isBetween Us is a personal MacBook belonging to its maker, used as a place to keep memories and as a medium to communicate with the person he loves most. Inside are various photos, songs, notes, messages, and small things from the journey they went through together — a journey that left many meaningful memories for its maker. Every app, photo, song, message, and note in it holds a story and keeps the memory of the moments they once shared.
Why a MacBookThis web experience is made to resemble a personal MacBook belonging to its maker, because that device played an important role in connecting its maker with someone he loves dearly. More than just a macOS simulation, every app inside is filled with personal content holding pieces of his journey, moments, and stories. This project was developed from macweb.dev, a work by gaminghackintosh, then adapted into a more personal experience.
